Astro Stars

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Astro Stars is a personal sky instrument for iPhone and Apple Watch. Positions and readings are computed on your device. This page explains what is processed.

Data calculated on device

Natal chart, transits, the day’s sky facts, and related readings are calculated locally from information you enter (name, birth date/time/place, preferences).

Storage

  • Profiles and journal: stored with Apple frameworks on your device; iCloud sync may apply if you enabled it for the app.
  • Preferences (tabs, notifications, display options): local UserDefaults / settings, optionally mirrored via iCloud Key-Value when available.

Account

You can use the app as a guest. If you use Sign in with Apple, the Apple identifier provided to the app is only used to restore your account mode.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions go through StoreKit / the App Store. Apple processes payment; we do not receive your card details.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the system schedules local reminders related to your sky. No third-party server is required for those local reminders.

No advertising tracking

Astro Stars does not do cross-app advertising tracking (App Tracking Transparency is not used for ads). The App Store privacy label reflects Data Not Collected for tracking.

On-device AI

Some conversational features may use on-device models (Apple Intelligence / local models). In those modes, content stays on the device. OpenAI is optional and only used if you paste your own API key.
